# Demo media provenance

- Visuals: three original, AI-generated landscape images created specifically for the Torudake Reel demo on 2026-08-10. They depict a rain-washed city, a quiet coastline, and a riverside sunset. No social-media footage, third-party logo, or copied text is embedded.
- Motion: the stills are animated and crossfaded in the reproducible HyperFrames composition under `scripts/demo-composition/`.
- Audio: one Japanese narration take generated with the same `gpt-realtime-2.1-mini` model family used by the production narration route. The temporary generation key was revoked immediately after the local WAV was saved.
- Captions: rendered by the Torudake Reel website UI at playback time so they can stay editable and accessible; they are not burned into the MP4.
- Final media: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, 10.4 seconds.
